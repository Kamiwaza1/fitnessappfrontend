Barbell Bench Press

Chest Compound

How to Perform

  1. Lie flat on your back on a bench. Grip the bar with hands just wider than shoulder-width.
  2. Squeeze your shoulder blades together and plant your feet firmly on the ground.
  3. Slowly lower the bar to your mid-chest while inhaling.
  4. Push the bar back to the starting position as you exhale, locking your elbows at the top.

Target Muscles

Primary

Pectoralis Major

Secondary

Triceps, Anterior Deltoids
